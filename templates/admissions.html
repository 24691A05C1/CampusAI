📁 Document Preparation Checklist

Keep these commonly requested documents ready. Requirements may differ by college and admission category.

  • 10th class marksheet
  • Intermediate / 12th class marksheets
  • Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Study certificates
  • Identity proof
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Entrance examination rank card and hall ticket, if applicable
  • Caste certificate, if applicable
  • Income certificate, if applicable
  • Other documents mentioned in the official notification

🎓 Final Admission Steps

  1. Check your seat allotment or admission offer.
  2. Read the official admission instructions carefully.
  3. Verify the fee amount and payment procedure.
  4. Submit the required documents for verification.
  5. Complete payment only through officially approved channels.
  6. Report to the college on the date mentioned in the admission instructions.
